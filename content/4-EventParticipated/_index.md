During my internship, I participated in one events. Each one was a memorable experience that provided new, interesting, and useful knowledge, along with gifts and wonderful moments.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)  
&emsp;**Event Name:** Event Meet up 06-06-2026

&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00, June 06, 2026  

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Content:** The meetup on June 6, 2026, brought together numerous speakers to share insights on topics related to Cloud, DevOps, and modern software development—including Docker, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Event-Driven Architecture, GraphRAG (using Amazon Bedrock and Neptune), AWS WebSockets, Amazon Q Developer, and NIDS systems on AWS. A key highlight of the event was Docker; sessions helped attendees understand the differences between Virtual Machines and containers, the application containerization process, Dockerfile optimization, and the role of Docker in building modern systems and implementing CI/CD pipelines.

&emsp;**Results or values ​​achieved:** Following the meetup, I gained a clearer understanding of modern system design principles and learned how to choose between VMs and containers based on specific project requirements. I also acquired foundational knowledge of Docker, DDD, and Event-Driven Architecture. Furthermore, I gained insights into applying Docker to real-world projects—such as standardizing development environments, facilitating CI/CD implementation, and optimizing team workflows—thereby better preparing myself for a career path in Cloud and DevOps.
