### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always willing to help whenever I encounter difficulties, even outside working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, helping me focus better. However, I think it would be nice to have more social gatherings or team bonding activities to strengthen relationships.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
My mentor provided detailed guidance, explained concepts clearly whenever I had questions, and always encouraged me to ask whenever I was unsure. The administrative team was very supportive with procedures, documentation, and everything I needed to work efficiently. I especially appreciated that my mentor encouraged me to think critically and solve problems on my own rather than simply giving me the answers.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The tasks assigned to me matched the knowledge I had learned at university while also exposing me to new areas I had not encountered before. This allowed me to reinforce my foundation and gain valuable practical skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During my internship, I developed many new skills, such as using project management tools, teamwork, and professional communication in the workplace. My mentor also shared valuable practical experience that helped me better shape my career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is very positive. Everyone treats each other with respect and works professionally while maintaining a friendly atmosphere. Whenever there was an urgent project, everyone collaborated and supported one another regardless of their position. This made me feel like I was truly part of the team, even though I was only an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company offered an internship allowance and provided flexible working arrangements when needed. In addition, being able to attend internal training sessions was one of the highlights of my internship experience.
